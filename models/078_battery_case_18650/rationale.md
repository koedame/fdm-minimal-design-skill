# 078 18650 battery case

A round case for four 18650 lithium-ion cells, Ø64 × 80, under a flush screw lid. The body and lid are the 12_threaded_jar pair (45°
flanked threads, pitch 4, the lid as wide as the body); inside, the lower part of the body is a solid rack with four bores on a 21 pitch.
Each cell stands in its own bore, 33 deep, so no two cells touch and nothing loose can reach their terminals while the lid is on. The lid's
inside stops 0.6 over a 70 long protected cell; a bare 65 cell stands in the same bore and has 5.6 to rise, still held upright.

## Review

No defects or violations found. Checked in particular: the cells clear the lid by 0.6 at the longest size (swept: free at +0.5, stopped
at +1.0); the lid seats on the body and its threads catch (swept as 049: lifting it 1.0 meets the body's thread, pushing it down 0.3
meets the seat, 0.2 sideways is free, 1.0 stops); the four bores leave 1.9 of wall between them and stay inside the neck's opening, so
the cells pass the neck.

Dos and Don'ts: one visible chamfer value; no radii apart from the round outline and the bores; no fasteners; the lid is the secondary
dark grey, no accent; plain outside, the grip only on the lid's upper band.

Use cases:
- **Put cells in:** unscrew the lid; drop each cell into a bore, either end up; the lead-in finds the bore.
- **Take cells out:** tip the case over a palm; the cells slide out of their bores (0.25 clearance). Between the cells and the neck there
  is no room for fingers, so they are tipped out, not picked.
- **Carry:** screw the lid on; each cell stays in its own bore, apart from the others and from anything else in the bag.
- **Place:** the flat bottom stands on 2776 mm², the centre of gravity low in the rack.
- **Print:** body bottom down: the bores are vertical, the shoulder under the neck is 45°, the threads have 45° flanks; lid top down. No
  supports.

verify.py: both printed parts valid, watertight, one solid each; no interference with the cells; contact area 2776 mm².

Compared with well-known products (SK 4, T 3, ET 66, SK 61, L 2): none is a container. A round screw-top tube is a common kind of
object; no product was used as a model.

Safety (lithium-ion cells): store only undamaged cells, charged to about half for long storage. Do not leave the case in a car or in the
sun: PLA softens at about 55 °C, so print it in PETG, and a hot cell is a fire risk in any case. The case keeps the cells apart and their
terminals covered; it does not make damaged or swollen cells safe and is not a fire enclosure. Keep it away from children.

Not verified:
