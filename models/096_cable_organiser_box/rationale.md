# 096 Cable organiser box

A box 144 × 96 × 64 for the desk or the floor behind it, that hides the slack of cables: the excess length is coiled inside and only the
ends come out. Four U notches of the same shape run down from the rim, three in the front wall for charging cables and one in the back for
a cord. They run to the rim, so a cable is laid in from above and the lid closes over it; the lid locates on a lip that hangs from its wall,
as 07_hinged_case. Two printed parts.

## Review

No defects or violations found. Checked in particular: the lid lifts free, sits on the rim and stops at 0.4 sideways in x and y on its lip;
the notches' outlines are chamfered on both faces of the wall and round the rim; the lip hangs from the thickened wall (one solid); with the
lid on, each notch is a closed U 12 high under the lip, so a cable in it is not pinched.

Dos and Don'ts: one visible chamfer value (C0.6 only on the bed faces); R12 with its concentric R9.48 and R7.55, and the notches' round
ends; one margin; one seam line; one notch shape repeated; plain off-white, no accent; no fasteners.

Use cases:
- **Fill:** set the box over the cables' slack with the lid off, coil the excess inside and lay each end into a notch from above.
- **Close:** set the lid on; the lip finds the body's wall, nothing to undo. A cable pulled from outside is held at the notch's round bottom.
- **Add or take a cable:** lift the lid, lift the cable out of its notch.
- **Print:** body bottom down, the notches open to the rim; lid top face down, the lip standing up. No supports.

verify.py: both parts valid, watertight; fits a 256 bed; the lid lifts free, stops on the rim when pushed down and at 0.4 sideways.

Heat: the box is for the slack of cables. A charger or a power strip inside it has no ventilation and warms the box; if you put one in, use
only a certified device rated for the load and check that it stays cool with the lid on. Printed PLA softens at about 55 °C.

Compared with well-known products in this style: radios, a pocket radio, a calculator and a loudspeaker; none is a box with
cable notches. A box with a lid and notches for cables is the common form of a cable organiser; no product was used as a model.
