# 092 Wall plant pocket

A 72 cube that hangs on two screws and hides a small plant in its 60 mm nursery pot (Japanese size 2, rim Ø60, 55 high). The floor is
closed: water that runs out of the pot stays in the pocket and does not run down the wall, and two ribs 4 high keep the pot out of it.
The back stands flat against the wall with its vertical corners cut back 1.2 at 45°, so the cube sits on a thin shadow line; the back
wall is thickened to 6.4 and two keyholes are cut into it from the wall side. One printed part, the 051_remote_holder_wall arrangement.

## Review

| Target | Problem | Kind | Fix |
|---|---|---|---|
| pocket | The rim was chamfered before the cavity was cut, so only its outer edge had C0.5 and the inner edge was left square | Violation | The rim is chamfered after the cavity and the back wall are in, both edges |

Dos and Don'ts: one visible chamfer value; R10 with its concentric R7.48 (the keyhole's round ends are on the hidden wall face); one margin;
no fasteners in sight (the keyholes open only to the wall); plain off-white, no accent.

Use cases:
- **Hang:** two screws 36 apart, heads 3 proud of the wall; put the empty pocket over them and drop it 6. Then set the pot in.
- **Water:** water the plant in place; up to about 15 ml runs out under the pot and stays below the ribs. More than that reaches the pot's
  base: lift the pot out and tip the pocket off its screws to empty it.
- **Take the pot out:** lift it by the plant's base; 1.5 clearance all round, the rim 10.6 below the pocket's rim.
- **Print:** bottom down; the back wall's step is 45°, the keyholes' head pockets have 45° seats and roofs. No supports.

verify.py: valid, watertight; fits a 256 bed; the pot's envelope (rim Ø60, base Ø45, 55 high, on the ribs) does not touch the pocket
(checked in the review, 0 mm³).

Compared with well-known products (SK 4, T 3, ET 66, SK 61, L 2): radios, a pocket radio, a calculator and a loudspeaker; none hangs on a
wall or holds a plant. A cube planter is the common form; no product was used as a model.

Safety (load, water): with a wet pot the pocket carries about 400 g, more with water standing in it; its strength has not been
calculated or tested. How well it holds depends on the material (PETG; PLA breaks down when it stays wet for months), the print (three
or more perimeters) and the wall: drive the screws into a stud, or into plugs or plasterboard anchors made for your wall. Do not hang
it over a bed, a sofa, a cot or electrical equipment, where a falling pot or dripping water can hurt someone or damage something.
Before you put the plant in, pull the empty pocket down and away from the wall by hand, harder than the watered plant weighs.

Not verified:
- Not printed on a real printer.
- Whether the floor and walls hold water without seeping between layers (PETG with 3 or more perimeters, or a sealant inside, if it weeps).
- The hold of the keyholes with a wet pot of about 400 g on your wall plugs.
