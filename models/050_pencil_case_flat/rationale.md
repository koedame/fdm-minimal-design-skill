# 050 Flat pencil case

A flat case, 200 × 64 × 24, for pencils and pens up to 190 long. It is 07_hinged_case built long and low: a base 16 high and a dark lid
8 high (2 : 1), joined along the back seam by seven knuckles on a 1.75 filament pin, held shut by two magnets at the front, and opened
from a finger scoop across the middle of the front seam. A lip round the front half of the lid locates it on the base; there the lid's
wall is thickened inside down to the rim, so the lip is part of the wall and not a band that would print loose.

## Review

| Target | Problem | Kind | Fix |
|---|---|---|---|
| lid | Found later: the lid's magnet pockets were cut before the lip was joined; the lip's wall, thickened inside down to the rim, filled 10.5 mm³ of each pocket and a Ø6 × 3 magnet did not fit | Defect | Pockets cut after the lip is joined; the lid and the magnet's Ø6.2 × 3.2 space no longer overlap |

Checked: the lip is joined to the lid (one solid), it clears the magnet posts, the lid swings free from closed to 150° and stops at
both ends (swept), the knuckles print on their 45° webs both ways up, and the case stays standing when open.

Use cases:
- **Assemble:** press the magnets into the posts, opposite poles facing; set the lid on the base and push the filament pin through the
  knuckles from one end; melt or glue its ends.
- **Open:** a fingertip in the scoop lifts the lid off the magnets; it swings back to 150° and the case stays standing (swept: free
  from 0° to −150°, stops at −175° and at +1°).
- **Close:** let the lid fall; the lip finds the base and the magnets pull it shut.
- **Print:** base bottom down, lid top down; the knuckles stand on 45° webs, the lip stands up as the continuation of the wall. No
  supports.

verify.py: all parts valid and watertight, one solid each, no interference, fit a 256 bed.

Compared with well-known products (SK 4, T 3, ET 66, SK 61, L 2): radios, a calculator and a loudspeaker; none is a case with a lid.
Hinged pencil cases are a common kind of object; no product was used as a model.

Safety (magnets): the magnets are small; keep the case away from small children who could swallow a magnet that comes loose, and glue
the magnets in if the press fit is loose.

Not verified:
- Not printed on a real printer.
- The magnets' pull through the 0.2 gap.
- The filament pin's wear after many openings.
