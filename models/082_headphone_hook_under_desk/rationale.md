# 082 Under-desk headphone hook

A hook for a pair of headphones, hung under a desk top: 40 wide, its side profile a C 72 deep and 72 high that opens toward the chair.
A flat plate is screwed to the underside of the desk with two wood screws; the hook's top block covers it, the plate sitting in a matching
pocket and its two vertical bosses in holes in the block. Two M3 screws come up from below through counterbores behind the stem, into
pilot holes in the bosses. From the chair the stem hides them, and the plate and its wood screws are covered, so no fastener shows. The
headband rests on the arm, in a rounded seat between the stem and a raised lip. The hook prints lying on its side, so every layer is the
whole C.

## Review

| Target | Problem | Kind | Fix |
|---|---|---|---|
| hook | The lip was 8 thick against 12 for the stem and arm, with a R3.8 top: one bar of the C a different thickness for no reason | Violation | Lip 12 thick with a R5.8 top; to keep 28 of opening over it the arm moved down to 72 under the desk, which makes the profile 72 square |

Dos and Don'ts: one visible chamfer value (C0.6 only on the bed side); three radii by role; one bar thickness; the lip's end nearly full
round; no fastener seen from the chair; off-white, no accent.

Use cases:
- **Mount:** screw the plate to the underside of the desk at the side of the knee space, its long side square to the desk edge and its
  front 20 or more behind the edge. Press the hook up over it (swept: free from 0 to 30 down; the plate stops it 0.5 up, 0.5 sideways and
  0.5 forward or back), then drive the two M3 screws up from below, behind the stem.
- **Hang the headphones:** lift the headband over the lip (28 of room under the top block) and let it drop into the seat. The seat is a
  flat 12 between two R6 curves, so the headband's padding rests on a surface, not an edge.
- **Load:** a pair of headphones weighs 200–400 g. It hangs from the two M3 screws in the bosses and through the plate on the two wood
  screws; the arm is a 12 × 40 bar 36 long.
- **Remove:** take out the two M3 screws and lower the hook; the plate stays.
- **Print:** hook on its side, no supports (the pocket's flanks are 45°; the boss holes, the clearance holes and the counterbores are
  teardrops pointing up); plate desk side down, the bosses standing up.

verify.py: hook and plate valid and watertight, one solid each; no interference; fit a 256 bed; every motion as recorded.

Compared with well-known products (SK 4, T 3, ET 66, SK 61, L 2): radios, a pocket radio, a calculator and a loudspeaker; none is a
hook. Under-desk headphone hooks are a common item; no product was used as a model.

Safety (load): the hook is made for one pair of headphones (200–400 g); its strength has not been calculated or tested, and it is not
for bags or anything heavier. Its hold depends on the material, on printing it on its side as given, on the M3 screws in the printed
bosses and on the desk: drive the wood screws into solid wood or a solid board, not into a glass, metal, hollow-core or thin-panel
top. Before use, pull down on the arm by hand, clearly harder than the headphones weigh, and check that nothing moves.
