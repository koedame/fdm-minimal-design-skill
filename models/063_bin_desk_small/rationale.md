# 063 Small desk bin

A square bin for paper scraps and pencil shavings, 96 × 96 × 128, small enough to stand on a desk. It is used bare or with a small bag
folded over the rim; a V groove runs round the outside 12 below the rim, and a rubber band round the folded bag drops into it and does
not ride up. Without a bag the groove is the one line on the walls. The floor's inner edge is rounded R3 so crumbs do not collect in the
corners. One part on a floating plinth.

## Review

No defects or violations found. Points looked at and kept:

| Target | Question | Result |
|---|---|---|
| bin | Does the groove read as a seam of a lid that is not there? | It sits at a reference (12 below the rim = the plan radius) and has a function (the band's seat); without a bag it is the only line. Kept |
| bin | Is the wall behind the groove thick enough? | 1.72 (4 lines) over 1.6 of height; the rest of the wall is 2.52 |
| bin | Does the groove print? | Its upper flank faces down at 45°, the lower one up; no support |

Dos and Don'ts: one visible chamfer value plus C0.6 at the bed and the plinth's 1.2; two radii (R12 outline and its concentric R9.48, R3
inside); no fasteners; no accent (nothing has a function to point at); the inside and the outside are one wall thickness.

Use cases:
- **Throw in:** the opening is 91 × 91 with no rim lip in the way; paper and shavings drop straight to the floor of the bin.
- **Bag:** fold a small bag over the rim and put a large rubber band round it at the groove; the band seats in the V and holds the bag
  when the bin is emptied.
- **Empty:** lift the bag out, or turn the bin over; the R3 floor edge leaves no corner for crumbs.
- **Place:** stands on 8130 mm² inside the plinth (92.5 × 92.5); the empty bin weighs about 90 g of PLA, low and square, and does not
  tip when paper is dropped in.
- **Print:** bottom down, no supports. Put the Z seam on a back corner in the slicer: the large flat sides show a seam more than the
  trays do.

verify.py: valid, watertight, one solid; contact area 8130 mm².

Compared with well-known products (SK 4, T 3, ET 66, SK 61, L 2): none of them is a container; they are housings with controls,
grilles or perforation. Square desk bins are a common kind of object; no product was used as a model.

Safety: the bin has no batteries, LEDs, magnets or heat of its own. PLA softens at about 60 °C: do not put hot ash or matches in it.
