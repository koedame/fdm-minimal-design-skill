# 039 Napkin holder

An upright napkin holder for the table, 128 long × 48 × 96 high (8 : 3 : 6): two walls on a base, the napkins stood on edge in the slot
between them. The whole holder is one U profile run straight through its length, so it prints lying on one end: every layer is the
complete U, the walls are as strong as the plastic along their height, and nothing overhangs. The base floats on a plinth set 1.2
inward at 45° along both long sides (03 tray); inside, the floor runs into the walls on a cove, so the bottom napkin is not creased into
a corner.

Use cases:
- **Fill:** stand a stack of folded napkins on edge in the slot; they lean on one wall as the stack gets shorter.
- **Take one:** pinch the front napkin above the walls and pull up.
- **Wipe:** the inside has no corner (R8 cove); the outside is flat faces with C0.5 edges.
- **Print:** lying on one end, 128 high (fits the A1 mini's 180), no supports; the foot recesses have 45° points.

verify.py: valid and watertight, fits a 256 bed (128 high as printed); ground contact 5440 mm².

Compared with well-known products in this style: radios, a pocket radio, a calculator and a loudspeaker; none is a

Safety (kitchen): the holder is not for food contact and not for the dishwasher. PLA softens at about 55–60 °C; keep it away from
the hob and hot pans, or print it in PETG.
