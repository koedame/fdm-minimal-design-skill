# 084 Planter on a tripod

A round cache pot, Ø104 × 96, on three tapered legs that lift it 48 off the floor: 144 high in all. It holds a 9 cm nursery pot. Each leg
is a flat plate, 24 wide under the pot and 12 at the foot, that prints lying on its side with its outline on the bed, so the layers run
along the leg; two Ø4 steel dowels and glue join its flat top to the pot's 8 thick floor. The seam is a V line, C0.5 on both sides. The pot
is off-white, the legs the secondary dark grey.

## Review

| Target | Problem | Kind | Fix |
|---|---|---|---|
| pot, leg | The bed faces had C0.5 like the rest: the pot's bottom edge and every edge of the face the leg lies on in the print | Violation | C0.6 on those edges, against elephant's foot |
| pot | The dowel holes open on the bed face; elephant's foot would narrow their mouths and the dowels would not go in | Defect | C0.6 cone round each hole's mouth (it also takes the excess glue) |

Dos and Don'ts: one visible chamfer value (C0.6 only on the bed faces); the plan is a circle, the legs are straight-sided; the feet are
flat with a chamfer; the seam is one V line under the pot; no fastener shows (the dowels are inside); pot off-white, legs dark grey, no
accent.

Use cases:
- **Place:** stands on three feet, so it does not rock on an uneven floor; ground contact 363 mm² spread over 115.6 × 100.1. The feet sit
  at about r 58, so the stance is a triangle whose inscribed circle has a radius of about 30; with a wet 9 cm pot the centre of gravity is
  about 95 up, so it tips at about 17°. Fine on a shelf or the floor, not where it is pulled or bumped.
- **Plant:** set the nursery pot in; there is no drainage hole, so water what the plant takes and tip out what collects in the pot.
- **Assemble:** glue the dowels into the pot's holes (they stand 6 out), then glue each leg onto its pair; the dowels set the leg's place
  and angle.
- **Print:** pot bottom down (a plain cylinder, the dowel holes vertical). Legs on their side, the outline on the bed: the dowel holes are
  horizontal and are teardrops pointing up. No supports.

verify.py: pot and legs valid and watertight, three separate legs; no interference with the dowels; fit a 256 bed.

Safety: the pot holds water. Check that no water stands on furniture that it can damage; the printed pot is not proven watertight.
