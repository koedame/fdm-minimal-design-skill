# 020 Divided valet tray

A low tray (192 × 128 × 24) for the things emptied from pockets at the door or on a desk. A long divider splits it 2 : 1 front to
back; the back row is split into three equal cells. It is built as 03_tray: one body standing on a 45° undercut plinth, so it floats
on a shadow line, with the dividers 4 below the rim and R6 where the floors meet the walls.

## Review

No defects or violations found.

Use cases:
- **Empty pockets:** the phone goes in the front cell along its length, the wallet beside it; keys, coins and a watch go in the back
  cells, which are behind the phone and not under the hand that reaches for it.
- **Pick up:** the phone is gripped at its ends (the cell is 20 longer than a large phone at each end); a coin is pushed into the R6
  curve and tipped up; the 20 mm dividers do not hide the back cells from a standing view.
- **Clean:** the R6 floors leave no corner for dust; turn it over and tap it out.
- **Print:** bottom down; the plinth's 45° undercut and the vertical walls need no support. Dark grey hides scuffs from keys.

verify.py: valid and watertight, fits a 256 bed, ground contact 22952 mm² over 188.5 × 124.5.

re-divides 03_tray and was not modelled on a product.
